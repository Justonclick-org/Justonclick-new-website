Justonclick HRMS & Payroll

In-house product — designed, built, and operated by Justonclick for its own HR & payroll operations.

IndustryHuman Resources & Payroll Technology (horizontal, cross-industry)
Live Linkhttps://hrms.justonclick.in/
Business Problem

Most growing businesses start HR and payroll on spreadsheets and disconnected biometric devices. That approach works at a small scale, but creates real risk as headcount grows: manual reconciliation errors, missed statutory compliance updates (PF, ESI, TDS), no self-service for employees, and no way to answer a headcount or cost question quickly.

Objectives
  • 01Bring attendance, leave, payroll, and compliance into a single system
  • 02Automate statutory compliance rather than track it manually
  • 03Give employees a modern self-service experience
  • 04Provide leadership with real-time HR and cost visibility
Solution Architecture

A single platform covering the full employee lifecycle — from onboarding through payroll to performance management — with statutory compliance rules built into every payroll run rather than applied manually after the fact.

Technology Stack
ReactNode.jsPostgreSQLAndroid appBiometric & attendance integrations
UI Screens
Development Process
1Map HR policies & statutory rules
2Design payroll & compliance engine
3Build attendance, payroll & self-service
4Roll out internally & iterate

Compliance rules live in configuration rather than code, so a statutory change doesn't have to wait for a new release.

Challenges

Designing a compliance rule engine flexible enough to handle statutory changes (PF, ESI, TDS) without requiring a code change for every regulatory update.

Results
  • Payroll processing time cut from days to hours
  • Statutory compliance risk reduced through built-in rule engines, applied automatically every cycle
  • HR data centralized in one auditable system instead of scattered spreadsheets
  • Every employee given a modern self-service experience, reducing routine HR queries

Astra Dental Clinic

Astra Dental Clinic — a cosmetic and restorative dental clinic in Chembur, Mumbai, led by Dr. Amit Pawar (BDS, MDS).

IndustryHealthcare — Dental Clinic
Live Linkhttps://astradental.co.in/
Business Problem

Patients choosing a dentist compare clinics online before they ever call. Astra Dental needed a website that reflected the care patients experience in the clinic, explained each treatment clearly, ranked for people searching for a dentist in Chembur, and made booking a visit as quick as sending a message.

Objectives
  • 01Present the clinic, the doctor, and every treatment with clarity and credibility
  • 02Turn visitors into booked appointments with as little friction as possible
  • 03Be found by patients searching for dental care in Chembur and Mumbai
  • 04Deliver a fast, mobile-first site patients can use on any device
Solution Architecture

A React single-page site with a dedicated page for each treatment — implants, root canal, braces & aligners, cosmetic and paediatric dentistry, and smile makeovers — plus gallery, pricing, FAQs, team, and legal pages. The appointment form opens WhatsApp with the patient's details pre-filled, so the clinic receives booking requests where it already works, with no back-end to maintain.

Technology Stack
ReactViteReact RouterBootstrapWhatsApp bookingGoogle Analytics 4
UI Screens
Development Process
1Content & treatment structure
2Design
3Build & local SEO
4Launch

Each treatment got its own page with its own search metadata, so the site can rank for the specific treatment a patient is searching for, not only the clinic's name.

Challenges

Making booking effortless without adding a booking system the clinic would have to run — solved by handing the patient's details straight to WhatsApp, the channel the clinic already answers every day.

Results
  • Live production website at astradental.co.in, serving as the clinic's primary web presence
  • Patients can book an appointment or start a WhatsApp consultation from any page
  • Dedicated, search-optimized pages for each treatment, with local SEO for Chembur and Mumbai
  • Responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile

Local Credit Solutions

Local Credit Solutions — a digital KYC verification and fraud-prevention provider for businesses.

IndustryFinancial Services — KYC Verification & Fraud Prevention
Live Linkhttps://lcsolution.in/
Business Problem

Identity verification and fraud-prevention services carry a higher trust bar than most other categories. Local Credit Solutions needed visitors to understand what was being offered quickly and clearly, and to feel confident before sharing any personal or financial information — without an existing site built to convey that credibility.

Objectives
  • 01Design and build a professional, trustworthy web presence
  • 02Explain KYC and fraud-prevention services in plain, trust-building language
  • 03Deliver a responsive, production-ready site across devices
  • 04Launch and hand over the finished site to the client's team
Solution Architecture

A custom business website built from the ground up — no generic template — with content structured around the client's KYC verification and fraud-prevention services and a clear path to the client login.

Technology Stack
React front endResponsive design system
UI Screens
Development Process
1Design & content structure
2Build
3Launch
4Handover

Content structure was settled before visual design, so the site reads clearly for first-time visitors.

Challenges

Explaining KYC verification and fraud-prevention services so a non-technical audience could navigate confidently, without the site feeling like a sales pitch.

Results
  • Live production website at lcsolution.in, serving as the company's primary web presence
  • KYC verification and fraud-prevention services explained in plain, trust-building language
  • Responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • A clear route for existing customers to the client login
